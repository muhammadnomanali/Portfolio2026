Who I Am

I am a graduate student at Auburn University pursuing a master’s degree in Technical and Professional Communication. My work focuses on how communication functions across cultural, linguistic, and social boundaries, particularly in high-stakes digital environments.

I study how people interpret uncertainty, risk, and credibility online, and how communication can either reinforce or reduce barriers to understanding. Through an intercultural TPC lens, I examine how writers and designers can create more inclusive, accessible, and ethically responsible content that supports diverse audiences.

My research and practice center on the relationship between communication, trust, and action. I am especially interested in how thoughtful design and language choices can improve clarity, reduce harm, and foster meaningful engagement across different cultural contexts.

I am eager to connect with professionals and researchers who are working to make communication more equitable and effective in global and interdisciplinary settings. My goal is to contribute to spaces where communication is not only clear, but also culturally aware and socially responsible.
